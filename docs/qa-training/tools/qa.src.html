Verdict. The Hotel PMS and Restaurant POS work end to end on the local XAMPP installation. They behave as two separate systems joined only at the three designed integration points: POS charge to villa → folio, open restaurant checks block checkout, and checkout → housekeeping. All permission boundaries held under direct-URL and direct-API testing. Every money calculation checked in this audit matched the system's tax rules to the cent (examples in §7).

Before live use, two environment actions are required: (1) register the Laravel scheduler in Windows Task Scheduler — without it the automatic night audit, website-hold expiry and key-card expiry never run; (2) cache the configuration on the Windows/XAMPP server (php artisan config:cache) to stop intermittent HTTP 500 errors under concurrent requests. Three medium-severity product issues remain (§9): night-audit timing and audit trail, the booking-form summary ignoring a rate override, and the second-outlet shift form being hidden.

Not verifiable here. Real RFID locks and encoders, live OTA connections, a real payment gateway, WhatsApp Cloud API and SMS are not connected or not implemented. The key-card and payment results in this report come from the built-in simulator and sandbox and are labelled as such.

History of failures during the audit

The first harness run reported 43 failures. 38 were harness mistakes (for example, the form posts villas[<id>][selected]=1, and the harness had omitted selected) and were corrected in the harness. The rest were real defects, fixed and listed in §8. In the first browser pass, 6 steps failed because of script errors (selector quoting, a root-relative API URL) or unknown data state (two leftover demo checks on T6, and a cashier shift open at another outlet — which led to finding O-06). 13Night audit — code review

NightAuditService::run($date), called from Front desk → Run night audit (frontdesk.night_audit) or hms:night-audit, which is scheduled daily at 02:00:

  1. For every checked-in booking: RoomChargeService::postNights($b, $date + 1 day) posts every unposted night before the day after the business date, with 10% service and 8% tax and a rate factor for discounts. It is idempotent per booking-villa night (NA-03).
  2. Confirmed bookings with arrival < business date → markNoShow.
  3. Expire website holds; expire key cards past validity.
  4. generateStayovers($date + 1) for occupied villas not departing that day (count fixed in D-06).
  5. markAbsences($date − 1) for staff attendance.
  6. Saves the summary to setting night_audit_last only (O-04).

Timing (O-03): with the 02:00 schedule, the business date is the new calendar day. Running the audit before midnight — as in this test at 23:49 — gives the expected hotel-industry result.
